Our Story

Born from a passion for dogs and the ocean, SurfPup is more than a surf school—it's a lifestyle.

How It All Started

In 2015, our founder Sarah discovered that her Golden Retriever, Cooper, had a natural talent for balancing on her surfboard. What started as a fun weekend activity soon became a viral sensation and eventually, a professional academy.

Today, SurfPup has trained over 5,000 dogs across the country, helping them build confidence, strength, and an unbreakable bond with their owners.

World-Class Safety Standards

1:2 Trainer Ratio

We maintain a strict ratio of one trainer for every two dogs to ensure constant supervision.

First-Aid Certified

All trainers are certified in canine CPR and first-aid to handle any beach-side emergencies.

Weather Monitoring

We only conduct classes in safe ocean conditions. If waves are too high, we reschedule at no cost.
